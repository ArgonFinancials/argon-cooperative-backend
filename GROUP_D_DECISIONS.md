# Argon: four decisions needed before building (2026-10-07)

Four parts of the Argon cooperative platform have screens designed but no
rules agreed. Nothing will be built for any of them until these questions are
answered, because the rules are what the system enforces, and building on
guesses means members' money follows rules nobody chose.

Each section can be answered on its own. Please reply with answers or with who
should decide.

---

## 1. Projects (members funding a shared project). Owner: Product, with the society

1. **Is joining a project a binding commitment?** If a member pledges ₦50,000,
   is that an obligation, or an intention they can withdraw?
2. **If a member stops paying,** what happens to what they have already put
   in? Refunded? Kept for the project? Converted to savings?
3. **Funding from savings:** when a member commits part of their savings, is it
   locked (no longer withdrawable) until the project ends? If so, when is it
   released?
4. **Funding by loan:** does a project loan follow the society's normal loan
   product and approval, or does it have its own terms?
5. **If a project fails or is over-subscribed,** what happens to the money
   collected, and who decides?

## 2. Subscriptions and payroll collection. Owner: Product, Commercial, and the payment partner

1. **Mandates:** who authorises a standing collection from a member's account,
   and how does a member cancel it?
2. **A failed collection:** how many times is it retried, how far apart, and
   does a retry cost the member anything?
3. **Who is told when a collection fails** (the member, an officer, both), and
   how quickly?
4. **A member's channel:** a member is collected by exactly one route (payroll,
   subscription or cash). What happens when they move from one to another
   mid-month?
5. **A lapsed subscription:** is the missed amount owed (arrears the member
   must make up), or simply not collected?

## 3. Field collection and sync (officers recording cash offline). Owner: Product and Operations

1. **What may a field officer record offline:** amounts against existing
   members only, or new members too?
2. **When the offline record and the central record disagree,** who wins, and
   who decides when neither should?
3. **Evidence:** what must accompany a cash collection (a receipt number, a
   photo, a member's confirmation)?
4. **The design says a record that fails to sync three times becomes a
   "conflict" with a stated reason.** Is three the right number, and who
   resolves conflicts?

## 4. Insurance. Owner: Commercial and Legal

1. **Which products, from which underwriters?**
2. **Is cover compulsory** for some members (for example, borrowers), or
   always optional?
3. **Premiums:** collected how (from savings, alongside contributions,
   separately), and passed to the underwriter how often?
4. **Claims:** who files them, who decides, and what does the member see?
5. **Licensing:** does offering insurance make Argon a broker or agent, and
   what licence does that require?
